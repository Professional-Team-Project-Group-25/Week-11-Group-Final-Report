The Problem

Insurance fraud costs the UK over £1.1 billion every year — and traditional rule-based systems can't keep up.

⚖️ Severe class imbalance: Only 6% of Fraud Oracle claims are fraudulent. A naïve classifier predicting "genuine" always achieves 94% accuracy whilst detecting zero fraud.
🧠 Missed semantic signals: Structured tabular models ignore the narrative context of claims — the "how" and "why" that fraud patterns reveal in language.
🔗 Research gap: NLP + tabular fusion for insurance fraud detection remains largely unexplored in the literature.

Hybrid Architecture Deep Dive

How the NLP Pipeline Works

01

Narrative Template Engineering

Each structured claim row is converted to a natural language sentence using a hand-crafted template. This bridges tabular data and Transformer models.

"A 30-year-old customer from IN reported a
 Major Damage Single Vehicle Collision incident.
 Authorities were YES to the scene.
 Total claim: $71,710 incl. vehicle: $37,520."
02

DistilBERT Fine-Tuning

distilbert-base-uncased fine-tuned for binary sequence classification (fraud=1, genuine=0). 3 epochs, batch size 16, 800 training narratives. 40% fewer parameters than BERT-base, 60% faster inference.

distilbert_fraud_score ∈ [0, 1]
→ appended as 102nd feature column
03

XGBoost Meta-Learner

Trained on 102 features (101 tabular one-hot + distilbert_fraud_score). The meta-learner simultaneously exploits structured signals and the semantic fraud probability from DistilBERT.

n_estimators=100 · max_depth=4
eval_metric='logloss' · random_state=42
